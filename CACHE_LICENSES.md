<!-- Shiqi Zhang 和 Tuomas Virtanen 为配套论文整理了这些缓存，处理包括音频分段、特征提取、帧级和片段级标签转换、数据划分以及距离计算。 -->
Shiqi Zhang and Tuomas Virtanen prepared these caches for the [accompanying paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13571). Processing includes audio segmentation, feature extraction, frame-level and segment-level label conversion, dataset splitting, and distance computation.

<!-- 特征提取使用 Qiuqiang Kong、Yin Cao、Turab Iqbal、Yuxuan Wang、Wenwu Wang 和 Mark D. Plumbley 发布的 PANNs Cnn14_DecisionLevelMax 预训练权重，其许可为 CC BY 4.0。缓存不包含原始音频或预训练权重。 -->
Feature extraction used PANNs `Cnn14_DecisionLevelMax` [pretrained weights](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3987831) by Qiuqiang Kong, Yin Cao, Turab Iqbal, Yuxuan Wang, Wenwu Wang, and Mark D. Plumbley, released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Raw audio and pretrained weights are not included.
